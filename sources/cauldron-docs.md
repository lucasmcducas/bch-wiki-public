# Riften Labs — Cauldron DEX Docs (2026-07-10)

## What Cauldron is

> "Cauldron DEX is the flagship product of Riften Labs. Cauldron is a decentralized exchange on Bitcoin Cash that lets users swap and provide liquidity to native cash tokens."

## Motivation

- Bring DeFi to BCH
- Bring more liquidity to BCH network
- Grow the CashTokens ecosystem
- Onboard DeFi users and liquidity

## Philosophy

> "Our Philosophy when developing this DEX has been to develop the most easy-to-use and open DEX; where anyone can swap any token and anyone can provide liquidity."

> "To grow a DeFi infrastructure like Cauldron, we believe it is vital to operate on a non-custodial platform, free of censorship as well as have a doxed development team."

## Riften Labs ecosystem

- Cauldron — AMM DEX
- Moria — Liquity-style borrowing/redeeming/liquidations protocol
- Delphi — price oracle
- WizardConnect — wallet connection protocol
